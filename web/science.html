Explicitly nondimensional. This compiler supports 1–4 canonical pairs with positive constant masses and polynomial potentials. Use + − * /, parentheses, and integer powers 0–8; division requires a nonzero constant denominator. It rejects unknown symbols, unsupported expressions and invalid run parameters at their source locations. There is no physical-unit checking.


The method is kick–drift–kick with binary64 arithmetic. Its step is fixed; a finite output is not an accuracy guarantee. Energy error is generally nonzero. The native and browser tests use independent analytic gradients, harmonic solutions and a refined RK4 reference. Charts display emitted samples and may downsample their lines; exports retain every emitted row. Browser output is capped at 8 MiB; increase sample for long runs.


Literal syntax, unsupported models and resource bounds
Decimals use digits or digits.digits and unary minus; exponent notation, NaN and infinity literals are rejected. Constant declarations can reference earlier constants. Potentials may reference all declared coordinates and parameters. Power binds before unary minus; -x^2 means -(x^2). Functions, state-dependent division, momentum-dependent potentials, alternative kinetics, constraints and dissipation are unsupported. Limits: 32 parameters, 65,536 source bytes, 4,096 tokens, 32 nested parentheses or consecutive unary minuses, 128 bytes per literal, 8,192 expression nodes and 65,536 expanded potential/force AST nodes. Steps and sample stride are integers from 1 to 1,000,000, with sample ≤ steps. Nonfinite trajectories fail explicitly; partial output is incomplete.
