# Zig status at the source baseline

This is a history and source inspection, not a current Zig conformance receipt.
The baseline was `ac7e9ebfd75855e1b46a16e17b593fe5226ec4c5`.

* `8422935cb92249326da34afaeb7f576f58dce410` introduced an AIR-to-lang-AST backend; `bae4587f33ac837b13f9ae79430dab179b92f5bd` moved it into the separate `patches/zig/src/codegen/lang_ast.zig`. `patches/zig/manifest.yaml` pinned Zig 0.15.2; `patches/zig/001-lang-ast-piggyback.patch` hooked the emitter. `scripts/apply-patches.sh` and `scripts/extract-zig-ast.sh` applied and extracted it, and `scripts/test-zig-capture.sh` exercised the historical capture.
* `3b3988371b39252b0ce760880951d2d69b40662c` added the handwritten subset reader `zig_reader/zig_reader.zig`; `ff8813d04f96f9148b804842e46422ccd5ff2091` reported the Camp 6 captured-reader smoke tests. `designs/path_b_zig_reader.md` records those historical claims, the limited subset, and remaining gaps. They are not a present-day reproduction.
* `ea9543b576d23078ea4382e9ebb2882d6a18db37` abandoned the AIR approach in `TODO.md`; `c603556649bdb60b4407dce46694c75be68912ee` deleted the emitter, patches, reader, capture scripts, and `test_zig_complex.c`. The deletion commit is the authoritative inventory; use `git show --stat c603556` and `git show c603556^:PATH` for archived source.
* `designs/zig_ast_compatibility.md`, `designs/air_emitter.md`, and `designs/path_b_zig_reader.md` are explicitly historical. `designs/cast_node.md`, `designs/i128_support.md`, `designs/abi.md`, `designs/shape_types.md`, and `designs/stdlib_strategy.md` contain related design discussion, not current Zig frontend evidence. `archive/boot-go/` is an older Go bootstrap, not the deleted Zig importer. `conformance/zig/` is an independent oracle harness, not a candidate Zig compiler.
* `src/sexpr_reader.lang` still parses the shared core S-expression format, not Zig AIR. Its former AIR unsigned comparisons (`ult`, `ugt`, `ule`, `uge`) now reject with `use-explicit-numeric-comparison`; `docs/NUMERICS.md` specifies explicit typed replacements. Core AST is not a complete or version-pinned Zig AIR transport. There is no current producer preserving the necessary AIR types, memory model, constants and calling rules. Restoring the historical emitter without those boundaries would not establish Zig correctness.

The reader CLI gives an explicit missing-reader diagnostic for `zig`; an explicitly registered or embedded reader still takes precedence. The backend uses its language-neutral missing-reader diagnostic. Neither provides a Zig compiler or verifies a captured AIR smoke path.

Zig worked as an experimental AIR importer from February 2026 and a handwritten subset reader from March 2026; the AIR approach was abandoned in May and both were deleted in August because reproducing Zig's lowered memory and calling semantics was an open-ended task, while the reader covered only a narrow subset. Restoring Zig needs a maintained, tested frontend for a defined subset, not just the existing core S-expression parser.
